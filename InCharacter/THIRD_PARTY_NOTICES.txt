Warcraft Wiki — https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/List_of_emotes

Data/EmoteCatalog.lua records facts from the wiki's emote table: each emote's
command, aliases and whether the table marks it as animated and/or voiced.
Wiki text is not copied. Wiki content is available under CC BY-SA 4.0.

Charades — https://github.com/damesene/wow-charades

Wheel.lua, Actions.lua, Editor.lua, Bindings.xml and Charades_Camelot.toc
were inspected as behavior/API references. In Character's implementation and
UI are independently written, without Charades assets. The binding declaration's
runOnUp/keystate pattern and the client-token-to-DoEmote dispatch path follow
Charades; its MIT notice is retained here.
